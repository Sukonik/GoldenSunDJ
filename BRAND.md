# GoldenSunDJ — Brand Voice & Identity ☀️🎧

Adapted from the GoldenSunAI brand kit for music. GoldenSunDJ is a GoldenSunAI product, so it keeps the same mark, wordmark style and warmth, and speaks in a music-forward voice.

## Positioning
GoldenSunDJ is a small, unusually capable AI-native music studio in your pocket: get out, record, sample, mix and play, with AI tools like Suno in your corner. Not a generic DJ app.

## Brand attributes
Warm · Optimistic · Technical · Inventive · Human-directed · Music-forward · Retro-future (Y2K chrome) · Practical · Outdoors-minded

## Visual language
- **Primary symbol:** the GoldenSunAI "G" inside a solar / compass-like sunburst, unchanged.
- **Wordmark:** `Golden` + **`Sun`** (accent) + `DJ`, set in Outfit 800, same treatment as `GoldenSunAI`.
- **Primary colors:** golden yellow, amber, deep brown / black.
- **Themes:** headphone and CD colorways. Gold Disc (default), Silver CD, Rainbow CD, Cobalt Disc, Studio Black, Pink Cans, Mint Buds, Sunset Cans. Each theme is three colors: primary, highlight, deep.
- **Type:** Outfit for display, Inter for text, mono for small labels.
- **Surface:** black with soft theme-colored glow, glass-like panels, chrome-edged pill buttons, no heavy motion.

## Logo system
1. Black & white mark: favicon, high-legibility use.
2. Gold 2D mark: primary branded mark (`assets/brand/emblem-gold-*.png`, transparent background).
3. Pixel forest + water version: Team GoldenSun / community / social banner.
4. Glowing forest version: launches, announcements, major milestones.

## Core copy
GoldenSunDJ makes it easy to record, sample, mix and play, wherever you are.

**Short:** Software for music, movement and everyday sound.

## Development philosophy
We don't just build tools that use AI.
We make music with AI, and keep a human in charge.

## Product philosophy
Understand → Organize → Discover → Act, tuned for music:

**Get out → Record → Produce → Master → Distribute → Mix → Play → Get found.**

## Voice
Short, friendly and practical. Say what to do next ("Press Record", "Next: Sampler"). Lead with the music, not the technology. No hype, no jargon.
